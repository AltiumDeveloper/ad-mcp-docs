# Design

Two tools open almost every conversation: `ad_design_project_list` and
`ad_design_projectvariant_list`. Each reports what is open and which one is currently
selected — the projects in Altium Designer, and the variants of a project.
[The session flow: projects and variants](../how-it-works.md#the-session-flow-projects-and-variants)
explains why the variant matters.
