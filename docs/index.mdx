# Altium Designer MCP Server

The **Altium Designer MCP Server** is an extension for Altium Designer that lets an AI
assistant — such as Claude — read and act on the design you currently have open.

Without it, an AI chat knows nothing about your board. You would have to describe your
circuit in words, or paste in netlists and screenshots by hand. With it, you can simply
ask:

> *"Which pins of U3 are unconnected?"*
>
> *"Are there any nets driven by two outputs?"*
>
> *"Add a comment on R31 explaining that this divider holds the reset pin low."*

and the assistant reads the answer directly out of your live design.

**MCP** stands for *Model Context Protocol*. It is an open, industry-standard way for
applications to expose their data to AI assistants. You do not need to understand the
protocol itself — think of it as a cable between Altium Designer and your AI chat window.

## What you can do with it

<div className="bc-grid">

[<i className="bc-icon bc-schematic"></i><strong>Design &amp; schematic data</strong> <span>Components, nets, connectivity, pin functions, net classes, differential pairs, design variants, sheet hierarchy, component parameters (MPN, manufacturer, datasheet links, footprint).</span>](./tools/schematic.md)

[<i className="bc-icon bc-erc"></i><strong>Design review (ERC)</strong> <span>Run a compile / Electrical Rule Check, summarise violations, drill into individual ones, and highlight the offending objects on the sheet.</span>](./tools/erc.md)

[<i className="bc-icon bc-history"></i><strong>History &amp; revisions</strong> <span>For a project managed in a workspace: what happened to it (commits, releases, copies), what a given commit changed, and the project overview or a query re-run against a committed revision instead of your working copy.</span>](./tools/history.md)

[<i className="bc-icon bc-comments"></i><strong>Collaboration</strong> <span>Create, read, reply to, assign, resolve, and delete comments on schematic objects — the same comments your colleagues see in Altium 365.</span>](./tools/comments.md)

[<i className="bc-icon bc-symbols"></i><strong>Schematic symbols</strong> <span>Create a symbol from a part number or a datasheet, validate an existing one against its datasheet or your house style, and update it in place — pin names, electrical types, grouping, multi-part splits.</span>](./symbols.md)

[<i className="bc-icon bc-editing"></i><strong>Schematic editing (experimental)</strong> <span>Place parts, draw wires, add net labels and ports, move or remove what is on a sheet, replicate a channel, add a sheet, and check a sheet against an intended netlist. Off by default.</span>](./tools/editing.md)

</div>
